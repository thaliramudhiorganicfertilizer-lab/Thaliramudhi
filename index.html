04INSIDE THE FAA INGREDIENT MIX

Simple ingredients.
Carefully prepared.

These ingredients are listed in the supplied Fish Amino Acid poster. Here is how each may contribute to the fermented mix and plant nutrition.

01
Fish-based ingredientsA source of protein and organic nitrogen after hydrolysis or fermentation; finished-product levels vary.
02
CurdCan introduce lactic cultures that help ferment the mixture.
03
EggAdds protein-rich organic material to the ferment; plant-available nutrients depend on processing.
04
Ripe bananaContributes fruit sugars and plant material; any potassium contribution depends on the final fermented product.
05
Jaggery / brown sugarProvides fermentable carbohydrates for microbes.

These are general ingredient roles, not a guaranteed analysis of this product. Ask for the finished product label and dilution instructions; avoid applying raw ingredients directly to plants.
